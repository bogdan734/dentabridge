# Landscape and claims: AI dental pre-consultation + teledentistry + travel quote
Checked 2026-10-25 (8 searches, 6 fetches). Tags: P = primary, S = secondary, "via summary" = page seen only as a search-tool summary, not opened, "older" = source dated before 2025-10-25. Not legal advice.

## 1) Question and constraints
Who already offers patient-photo dental triage, teledentistry, dental-tourism quotes and clinician-side dental AI (commercial and on GitHub); what white space can a public, non-commercial, country-agnostic portfolio demo credibly occupy (open multimodal model, live translated call, treatment + flight quote, booking); and what must its outputs, disclaimers and privacy handling say so it is not presented as a diagnostic device (FDA, EU MDR/AI Act, UK MHRA, GDPR/UK GDPR)? Constraints: student budget, public/synthetic data only, reproducible from the repo, EN+RU UI, fictional demo clinics.

## 3) Recommendation: adapt and build, reuse nothing as-is
Build the integration layer (photo triage -> translated call -> treatment + flight quote -> booking) yourself and adapt open data/baselines (MetaDent, AlphaDent, DENTEX; licences unchecked) for the fine-tune; no existing repo is safe to reuse.
Why: no source found combining these steps, GitHub teledentistry/tourism/VQA repos top out at 2 stars and the top dental repos at 340, and clinician-side leaders are closed FDA-cleared radiograph tools; the open slot is a reproducible, honestly evaluated VLM for patient-taken photos inside a research-only end-to-end demo.
It must NOT claim: diagnosis, clinical validity or parity with dentists, any FDA/CE/MHRA/HIPAA/GDPR status, real clinics/prices/flights, medical-grade translation, or replacing a dentist.

## 4) Improvements worth trying
- README and first screen must say: research/education demo, not a medical device, not medical advice, not for diagnosis, treatment or emergencies; not cleared or reviewed by FDA, an EU notified body or MHRA; public/synthetic data only, do not upload real patient or third-party images; clinics, prices, flights and bookings are fictional; AI findings, translation and voice can be wrong; no clinical validation (state data, n, metrics, limits).
- Output wording: "image observations" plus a "demo urgency hint" with uncertainty and "cannot rule out"; no verdict sentences ("you have caries"); always "see a dentist". Avoid in README/UI: diagnose, detects disease, clinical-grade, FDA/HIPAA/GDPR compliant, replaces your dentist.
- Make the honest evaluation the product: per-finding sensitivity/specificity with CIs, calibration, abstention rate, failure gallery, patient-phone vs clinic-camera shift, compared with the weak public baselines above (57%/100% at n=6; MetaDent <70% VQA); model card with out-of-scope uses.
- Privacy defaults: sample images first; opt-in uploads behind a separate explicit-consent checkbox naming health data; strip EXIF; auto-delete (e.g. 1 h); no accounts, analytics or training on uploads; write "designed to minimise data", never "compliant".
- Quote engine: show a range with assumptions and multi-trip cases (implants), as Dental Trip Savings does ("estimates, not quotes"); tag every price "illustrative".
- Label AI voice and translation in the call UI (AI Act Art. 50); confirm the exact carve-outs before relying on them.

## 5) Open questions for the owner
1. Real user uploads, or sample/synthetic images plus in-browser inference only? (decides GDPR scope)
2. Where do inference and storage run (EU/UK/US), and on which hosted services?
3. Flights: live fare API or mocked data? Is "booking tickets" a mock? (selling tickets is regulated)
4. Will released weights stay open if training data carries non-commercial terms (licences unchecked)?
5. Will a licensed dentist review labels and wording (no real patients involved)?
Lawyer flags if ever real (not researched): device classification and intended-use statements; DPIA, controller/processor roles, transfers; call-recording consent; cross-border practice and clinic liability; ticketing/package-travel rules; consumer-law claims; minors.
