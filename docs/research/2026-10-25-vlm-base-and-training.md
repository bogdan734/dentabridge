# VLM base model and training recipe for dental pre-consultation (research brief, 2026-10-25, updated for $0 compute and a <=4B training cap)
## 1) Question and constraints
Which open VLM (<=4B for training; larger models only as zero-shot baselines) should be LoRA/QLoRA-tuned to turn a tooth photo into JSON findings, a patient-friendly explanation (EN+RU), a triage level and an abstention ("photo unusable / uncertain, see a dentist") as decision support, not diagnosis? Constraints: $0 compute (free tiers and the M3 Max 36 GB only), public or synthetic data only, publishable reproducible weights, baselines served through Ollama. Legend: P = primary page, S = secondary or search excerpt, [n] = Sources, UNVERIFIED = not confirmed this session (all 12 searches and 9 fetches used); hours and images/hour are my estimates, not benchmarks.

## 3) Recommendation
- ADAPT, do not build: LoRA-tune Qwen3-VL-4B (2B for Mac runs) on Kaggle T4 / Colab T4 with Modal credits for H100 bursts, targets = JSON + explanation + triage + abstain, ablated against Design A and the zero-shot Ollama qwen3-vl:4b; cost $0 (lean run 2-3 h on a T4, full run 9-18 h inside Kaggle's 30 h/week; estimates).
- Why: (1) Ollama-native vision tag, documented mlx-vlm QLoRA path and a plain transformer that behaves on T4s, so the free, local and Ollama constraints all hold (Qwen3.5's Ollama vision is disputed); (2) Apache-2.0 (verify the card) lets you publish weights, MedGemma's Terms add pass-through, termination and deletion duties; (3) no model has dental evidence, so the decider is our own patient-level held-out set.
- Fallbacks: (1) Qwen3.5-4B (newer, Apache-2.0 verified, free Unsloth vision notebooks) once its Ollama-vision, fp16-on-T4 and mlx-vlm pilots pass; (2) MedGemma 1.5 4B (medical priors, HAI-DEF Terms, English-evaluated); (3) Gemma 4 E4B (Apache-2.0, but 4.5B effective / about 8B total is over the cap if counted by total).
## 4) Improvements worth trying
- Round trip first ($0, 30 min): untuned Qwen3-VL-4B + 20-step LoRA -> merge -> `ollama create` from the merged Safetensors dir -> image prompt. If vision is lost, serve the tuned model with llama.cpp or mlx-vlm and keep Ollama for baselines.
- Recipe start: Unsloth's Qwen3.5 example defaults (P): r=16, alpha=16, dropout 0, all-linear, batch 1 x grad-accum 4, adamw_8bit, max_seq 2048, vision and language layers both trained; lr and epochs not given (community 1e-4..2e-4, 2-3 epochs, early stop: UNVERIFIED). Ablate vision tower frozen vs trained; loss on answer tokens only; images 448-672 px; thinking off.
- Triage as a dentist-reviewed rule table over validated findings (VLM only explains and abstains), plus an image-quality gate trained on synthetic blur/dark/no-teeth/non-dental negatives (15-20% of data).
- Eval hygiene: split by patient/source, dedupe near-duplicates; macro-F1, per-class recall, under-triage rate, ECE and risk-coverage for abstention, hallucination rate, schema-valid rate under constrained decoding; fresh non-public test set (MedGemma card warns of contamination, P).
- Free-tier hygiene: 50-step pilot to replace my tokens/s guesses, checkpoint to the Hub or Kaggle output every 500 steps, lean yardstick first, one change per run.
- Russian and explanation quality: English output + translation layer vs bilingual fine-tune on a 100-sample rubric; judge text with a different-family LLM plus 50 human-checked samples (self-preference and verbosity bias); MedLayXplain as reference [8].
